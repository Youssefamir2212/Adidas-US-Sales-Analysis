## 📌 Project Overview

An end-to-end business intelligence project analyzing **9,648 Adidas transactions** 
across the United States between **2020 and 2021**.

The goal was to uncover profitability drivers, regional performance gaps, 
and sales channel efficiency to support data-driven business decisions.

## 💡 Key Insights

- 📉 **COVID Impact:** Sales dropped significantly in 2020
- 📈 **Recovery:** 294.23% YoY growth in 2021
- 🌍 **Regional Leader:** West region dominates with $270M
- 💻 **Online Efficiency:** Highest profit margin at 45% despite lowest sales volume
- 👟 **Star Product:** Men's Street Footwear leads in both sales and profitability
- 🏪 **Top Retailer:** West Gear outperforms Walmart and Amazon in profitability

---

## 📋 Recommendations

1. **Digital Expansion** — Increase Online channel investment to improve overall margins
2. **Inventory Focus** — Prioritize Men's Street Footwear production and marketing
3. **Regional Strategy** — Re-evaluate Midwest marketing to bridge the performance gap
